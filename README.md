Sunset Cover Maker — turn your photos into a multi-panel sunset poster in the style of GTA 6's cover art: a big hero panel plus smaller panels, bold Anton lettering, a Vice City sunset grade with a low sun and palm silhouettes, and one-click PNG export at 1080 × 1080, 1080 × 1350 and 1920 × 1080. Runs fully offline in a desktop browser; your photos never leave your machine. Pick a layout, crop and position each panel by dragging, write the title and subtitle, tune the sunset, export.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
# then open http://localhost:8080
```
